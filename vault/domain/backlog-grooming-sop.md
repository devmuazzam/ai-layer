### **1. Purpose**

The purpose of this SOP is to provide a clear and consistent process for conducting Backlog Grooming (Refinement). This ongoing activity ensures that the Product Backlog is always clear, ordered, and ready for future Sprints.

### **2. Scope**

This procedure applies to the Scrum Team, primarily the Product Owner and Developers, with facilitation support from the Scrum Master. The goal is to prepare high-priority backlog items for Sprint Planning.

### **3. Objective of Backlog Grooming**

Backlog Grooming helps the team:

* Keep the Product Backlog updated and prioritized
* Break down large items into smaller, clearer ones
* Estimate effort using story points or other methods
* Clarify requirements, acceptance criteria, and technical details
* Ensure items are ready to be pulled into the next Sprint

### **6. Format and Flow**

The Grooming session should follow this structure:

1. Review priority items
2. Clarify the requirement
3. Add or update acceptance criteria
4. Break down large stories
5. Estimate effort
6. Confirm readiness

This ensures the team enters Sprint Planning with a clean, clear, and actionable backlog.

### **7. What Backlog Grooming Is Not**

* Not a task assignment meeting
* Not a full Sprint Planning session
* Not a final approval for implementation

The goal is preparation, not commitment.

### **8. Expected Outcomes**

By the end of each Grooming session, the team should have:

* Refined and updated high-priority Product Backlog Items (PBIs)
* Clear descriptions and acceptance criteria
* Estimated story points
* PBIs broken down into smaller items, if needed
* PBIs marked as Ready for Sprint Planning

### **9. Scrum Master's Responsibilities**

* Schedule and facilitate Grooming sessions
* Ensure the session stays focused and within timebox
* Help the team apply good refinement practices
* Encourage open questions and cross-functional input
* Coach on Definition of Ready (if applicable)

### **10. Developers' Responsibilities**

* Participate in discussion and ask clarifying questions
* Help split large PBIs into smaller stories
* Estimate effort and identify technical risks
* Ensure stories are clear, doable, and testable
* Give input on how to make work more predictable

### **12. Success Criteria**

| Success Indicator | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Grooming happens regularly | Weekly or as needed |
| Stories are clear and estimated | Before Sprint Planning |
| Large stories are broken down | During the session |
| Items are marked "Ready for Sprint" | With team agreement |
| Backlog stays prioritized and manageable | Always |

### **13. Continuous Improvement**

Backlog Grooming practices should be reviewed during Sprint Retrospectives to improve:

* Story clarity and structure
* Estimation accuracy
* Team preparation and participation
* Use of Definition of Ready

The Scrum Master can adjust session format or frequency based on team feedback and backlog health.
