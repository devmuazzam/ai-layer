## **1. Purpose**

The purpose of this SOP is to define a clear and professional approach for conducting the Daily Scrum, a key Scrum event that supports daily alignment, promotes transparency, and helps the team inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal.

## **2. Scope**

This procedure applies to all members of the Scrum Team involved in product delivery during the Sprint. It includes Developers and Scrum master

## **3. Objective of the Daily Scrum**

The Daily Scrum is a 15-minute timeboxed event held each working day of the Sprint. Its goals are to:

* Inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal
* Adapt the Sprint Backlog as needed
* Promote self-organization and accountability
* Identify and raise any impediments early

## **6. Format and Flow**

A common and effective structure will be  followed is:

1. What did I work on yesterday?
2. What will I work on today?
3. Are there any blockers or issues?

## **7. What Daily Scrum is Not**

* Not for problem-solving
* Not a status meeting for the Scrum Master
* Not a full-team discussion — side conversations should happen after the meeting

## **8. Expected Outcomes**

By the end of each Daily Scrum:

* The team has a clear understanding of current progress
* Plans are adjusted as needed for the day
* Any blockers are mentioned by the developers

## **9. Scrum Master's Responsibilities**

* Ensure the event takes place and remains within the timebox
* Coach the team on keeping it focused and valuable
* Help remove impediments when raised
* Encourage a respectful atmosphere

## **10. Success Criteria**

| Success Indicator | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Daily Scrum happens consistently | Every day ( Mon - Friday ) |
| Meeting ends on time | 15 minutes |
| All Developers actively participate | 100% |
| Sprint Goal is referenced and understood | Clearly daily |
| Blockers raised early and followed up | In meeting & Immediately after the meeting |

## **11. Tips for an Effective Daily Scrum**

* Start on time, even if not everyone is present
* Use a visible board to guide discussion
* Stay focused on team goals
* End with clarity: everyone knows the plan for today
* Have follow-up discussions after the meeting

## **12. Daily Scrum Completion Checklist**

* Meeting started and ended on time (15-minute timebox)
* All team members participated
* Each member shared updates on:

    * Yesterday's progress
    * Today's planned work
    * Any impediments

* Shared board (e.g., Jira) was updated before or during the meeting
* Action items or blockers noted for follow-up (outside the meeting)
* Off-topic discussions were deferred appropriately

## **13. Daily Scrum Guidelines for Hybrid and Work from home**

To ensure effective Daily Scrum in remote or hybrid setups:

* Join on time via video call; use cameras for better engagement
* Keep it timeboxed to 15 minutes
* Each member answers:

    1. What did I do yesterday?
    2. What will I do today?
    3. Any blockers?

* Use a shared digital board (e.g., Jira) for visibility
* Mute when not speaking; one person speaks at a time
* Take deep-dive discussions offline

## **Conclusion**

The Daily Scrum is a vital event that enables the Scrum Team to inspect progress, adapt plans, and stay aligned toward achieving the Sprint Goal. All team members are expected to respect the time-boxed, participate actively, and uphold the values of focus, commitment, and openness.Whether working on-site or remotely, these guidelines ensure the Daily Scrum remains effective, inclusive, and aligned with Scrum principles.
