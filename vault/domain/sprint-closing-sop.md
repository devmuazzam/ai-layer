## **Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Sprint Closing**

Document Owner: Scrum Master
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: \[Insert Date\]
Applies To: Scrum Team (Developers, Scrum Master \]
Sprint Duration: 2 Weeks

### **1. Purpose**

This SOP defines a standardized process for conducting the Sprint Closing meeting. The aim is to formally close the sprint by reviewing completed work and aligning it with the sprint goal.

### **2. Scope**

This procedure applies to the entire Scrum team involved in the current sprint. It ensures transparency, completeness, and readiness for the next sprint.

### **3. Objective of Sprint Closing**

* Review all completed, in-progress, and uncompleted sprint items
* Confirm alignment with the defined Sprint Goal
* Mark the official end of the sprint in Jira
* Identify carried-over tasks

| Detail | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency | At the end of each sprint |

| Duration | Timeboxed to 30–45 minutes |

### **6. Format and Flow**

1. Validate whether it was achieved.
2. Brief showcase of delivered items.
3. Close completed stories, highlight carried-over items.
4. Understand root causes if applicable.
5. List all completed items.
6. Move unfinished work to backlog with reasons.
7. Transition to Next Sprint – Prep backlog for planning.

### **7. What Sprint Closing Is Not**

* Not a Retrospective
* Not a Planning Session
* Not a Blame Session

The focus is on completion and preparation, not feedback or planning.

### **8. Expected Outcomes**

* Sprint is formally marked as closed in Jira
* List of completed deliverables is shared
* Unfinished tickets are groomed and ready for reassessment
* Sprint metrics/reports prepared (optional)
* Team alignment on next steps

### **9. Scrum Master's Responsibilities**

* Schedule and lead the Sprint Closing
* Ensure the Jira board is up-to-date
* Facilitate discussion on sprint completeness
* Record carry-overs and blockers
* Prepare report or summary (if required)

### 10. Developers' Responsibilities

* Ensure ticket statuses are updated before the meeting
* Clarify reasons for any incomplete work
* Provide final input on sprint completeness
* Assist in identifying tasks to carry forward

### 11. Success Criteria

| Success Indicator | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Sprint is officially closed in Jira | Yes |
| Team agrees on completed vs. pending work | Yes |
| Carry-overs are tagged and documented | Yes |
| Board is clean for next sprint | Yes |
| Closing document is ready for review | Yes |

### **12. Continuous Improvement**

Feedback from Sprint Closing can inform better planning, estimation, and execution in future sprints. Consider capturing notes or insights for future reference or retrospective discussions.

### **13. Completion checklist for Sprint closing**

* All completed items meet the Definition of Done
* incomplete items are returned to the Product Backlog
* Stakeholders have completed User Acceptance Testing
* All feedback, bugs, and suggestions are logged
* Increment is ready for the Sprint Review demo
* All PBIs and tasks are marked as Done, Not Done, or Accepted
* Sprint is marked as Completed in Jira (or other tool)
* Sprint closing document is ready

## Conclusion

The Sprint Closing process helps the Scrum team officially end the sprint by checking what was done, what is still pending, and how it matches the sprint goal. It makes sure all work is reviewed, Jira is updated, and the team is ready for the next sprint. User Acceptance Testing (UAT) confirms that the work meets business needs. By following this process, the team stays clear, organized, and improves with every sprint.
