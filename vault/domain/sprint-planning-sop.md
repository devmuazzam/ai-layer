**Sprint Duration:** 2 Weeks

### **1. Purpose**

The purpose of this SOP is to define a consistent and effective approach to Sprint Planning, a core Scrum event that helps the team align on what will be delivered during the Sprint and how the work will be completed

### **2. Scope**

This procedure applies to all members of the Scrum Team involved in Sprint Planning, including the Developers and Scrum Master.

### **3. Objective of Sprint Planning**

Sprint Planning is a timeboxed event that takes place at the beginning of each Sprint. Its goals are to:

* Define a Sprint Goal
* Select Product Backlog Items  to work on
* Make a clear plan to get the work done

### **6. Format and Flow**

A common structure for Sprint Planning:

1. Select Product Backlog Items (PBIs)
2. Break work down into tasks
3. Provide Estimated hours & due date against each Tickets
4. Forecast what they can complete
5. Plan how they will complete it

### **7. Inputs Required**

* Product Backlog is ordered and refined
  Items are clear, prioritized, and ready to be worked on
* Team availability and capacity are known
  Everyone's availability is confirmed (e.g., vacations, meetings)
* Definition of Done (DoD) is clear
  Everyone understands what "done" means for a task or story

### **8. What Sprint Planning Is Not**

Sprint Planning is focused on planning this Sprint only, not the entire project. It's important to understand what it's not:

* **Not a full project planning meeting**
  We only plan for the upcoming Sprint, not for future months
* **Not a status review**
  This is not the time to report what's done; it's about planning what to do next
* **Not about assigning tasks top-down**
  The team decides together mutually  what to work on and how to do it

### **9. Expected Outcomes**

* A Sprint Goal that is clear, specific, and agreed on
* A list of backlog items the team plans to complete in the Sprint
* Each backlog item broken into smaller tasks (if needed)
* Tasks with:

    * Assigned team members
    * Estimated hours
    * Due dates

* A shared understanding of what to do and how it will be done

### **10. Scrum Master's Responsibilities**

* Ensure the meeting is scheduled and timeboxed
* Facilitate focused discussion
* Coach the team in planning practices
* Remove blockers

### **11. Developer  Responsibilities**

* Attend and actively participate in Sprint Planning
* Collaborate on defining the Sprint Goal
* Select and commit to deliverable Product Backlog Items (PBIs)
* Break PBIs into clear, manageable tasks
* Assign default story point of 1
* Add estimated hours and due dates to tasks
* Assign tasks to responsible team members
* Raise risks or blockers early
* Commit as a team to the Sprint Goal

### **12. Success Criteria**

| **Success Indicator** | **Target** |
| --- | --- |
| Sprint Planning happens at Sprint start | Every 2 weeks |
| Sprint Goal is clear and agreed upon | Defined and aligned |
| PBIs selected align with Sprint Goal | All are relevant and deliver value |
| Planning completed within 2-hour timebox | ≤ 2 hours |

### **13. Continuous Improvement**

Sprint Planning should be reviewed in every Sprint Retrospective to improve how the team plans future Sprints. The team should reflect on:

* Were the backlog items clear, refined, and ready to work on?
* Was the Sprint Goal specific and easy to understand?
* Was the amount of work realistic for the team's capacity?
* Did the team communicate and plan well together?
* Were the tasks broken down clearly and estimated correctly?

**13**. **Sprint Planning Completion — Final Checklist**

* All selected user stories are refined, have clear acceptance criteria, and are added to the sprint in Jira.
* Due dates and estimated hours are added to each task.
* Frontend, Backend tasks are clearly broken down
* Assignments of team members on Each task
* Team capacity matches the scope — no overload or unapproved mid-sprint additions.
* The Jira board is organized with correct start/end dates, tasks, and subtasks.
* Everyone knows and understands their responsibilities.
* Clear Goal is defined
* The designer to participate in the meeting, and all design-related queries have been clarified for the team

### **14 Remote or Hybrid Sprint Planning Guidelines**

* Use a shared board (e.g., Jira, Miro) and visible Sprint Backlog
* Ensure all team members are connected via video
* Mute when not speaking, one speaker at a time
* Share screen when selecting backlog items or estimating work
* Use a virtual timer to stay within the timebox

## **15. Conclusion**

Sprint Planning is a critical event that sets the foundation for a focused and successful Sprint. By collaboratively defining a clear Sprint Goal, selecting valuable Product Backlog Items, and creating a realistic plan, the Scrum Team ensures alignment, clarity, and shared ownership of outcomes.

Whether working in-person or remotely, all team members are expected to engage fully, communicate openly, and commit to delivering value within the Sprint. This SOP provides a consistent structure to help teams plan effectively and continuously improve their planning practices.
