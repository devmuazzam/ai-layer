# EPIC 16 — Safety & Emergency

## US-20 — Incident Reporting

**User Story**

As a field employee, I want to report an incident during an active ticket so that the applicable
Safety process receives the incident information.

**Acceptance Criteria**

* Incident Report action is accessible from the applicable active-ticket workflow.
* Incident information is associated with the applicable active ticket/job.
* Submitted incident information is made available to the applicable Safety process/team.
* Existing ticket progress is retained while the incident is reported.
* After completing the incident process, the employee can return to the applicable position in the ticket workflow.

---

## US-21 — SOS / Emergency Assistance

**User Story**

As a field employee, I want quick access to emergency assistance so that I can obtain help when
required.

**Acceptance Criteria**

* SOS/Emergency action is accessible during applicable ticket processing.
* SOS provides a direct **Call 911** action.
* SOS provides access to the applicable **Safety contact**.
* SOS provides access to the applicable **Crane Superintendent contact**.
* Applicable Safety contact routes to the configured/on-call Safety representative.
* Application displays applicable nearest-hospital information.
* Employee can call the applicable hospital.
* Employee can launch navigation to the applicable hospital.

# EPIC 17 — Employee Self-Service

## US-22 — Profile, Certifications & History

**User Story**

As a field employee, I want to access my employment-related information and historical records
from the mobile application.

**Acceptance Criteria**

* Employee can access their applicable **Profile information**.
* Employee can access applicable **Certification information and certification alerts**.
* Employee can upload applicable updated certification documentation for review.
* Uploaded certification information follows the applicable review/approval process.
* Employee can access applicable **Labor information**.
* Employee can view applicable completed/past tickets.
* Employee can access applicable historical checklist/inspection information.
* Employee can view applicable alerts/notifications associated with their field activity.
