### FR-FOP-028 — Certifications

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| ID | FR-FOP-028 |
| Module | Certifications |
| Title | Certifications |
| Description | The Application shall provide visibility into employee certifications supporting operational readiness. |
| Functional requirements | The Application shall display certification information, statuses, and expiration dates. The Application shall support certification alerts and viewing certification details. The Application shall support certification renewal submissions where applicable. The Application shall preserve certification history. |
| Business rules | Certification visibility shall respect user permissions. Certification notifications shall be configurable. Certification requirements shall be maintained by authorized personnel. |
| Acceptance criteria | **AC-001** Given certifications exist, When users review Certifications, Then certification information shall be displayed. **AC-002** Given certifications approach expiration, When notification thresholds are satisfied, Then certification alerts shall be generated. |
