## 9. Workflow-Affecting Business Rules

The following categories of business rules directly influence user experience and workflow behavior and shall remain referenced within the FRD:

* Mandatory activity enforcement.
* Workflow progression requirements.
* Signature requirements.
* Lunch validation visibility.
* Compliance validation outcomes.
* Escalation initiation conditions.
* Deficiency severity handling.
* Notification visibility rules.
* Certification alert visibility.

Detailed calculations and operational decision logic shall be documented within the corresponding Business Rules Specifications (BRS).
