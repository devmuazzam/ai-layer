## 5. Role Definitions

### 5.1 Operator / Oiler

Operator and Oiler users share a largely similar mobile experience.

Responsibilities include:

* Executing assigned tickets.
* Completing required inspections.
* Participating in JSA activities.
* Capturing labor-related information.
* Participating in compliance activities.
* Supporting customer interactions.

Additional Oiler responsibilities:

* Completes crane-related inspections.
* Completes inspections related to the Operator Load.
* Captures customer signatures.
* Completes the primary ticket completion workflow.
* Records Operator labor information where applicable.

### 5.2 Operator Load (Support Load 1)

Operator Load travels together with the crane assignment.

Responsibilities include:

* Managing assigned support load activities.
* Completing required inspections.
* Completing activities associated with the Operator Load.
* Managing the truck associated with the Operator Load.

### 5.3 Support Load 2 and Support Load 3

Support Load personnel are responsible for:

* Completing assigned ticket activities.
* Managing their assigned support load.
* Managing the truck associated with the support load.
* Completing required inspections.
* Participating in applicable compliance activities.

### 5.4 Third Man / Laborer

Responsibilities include:

* Executing assigned tickets.
* Completing assigned activities.
* Capturing labor information.
* Completing inspections where applicable.
* Participating in compliance activities.
