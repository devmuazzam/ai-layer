## 1. Executive Overview

The TCG Mobile Application provides a unified mobile experience for field personnel responsible for executing jobs, maintaining compliance, capturing labor, completing inspections, and interacting with customers.

The application is intended to replace fragmented operational processes currently distributed across Atoms Mobile, manual paperwork, and supporting applications. The future-state experience shall provide a guided workflow that supports employees throughout the lifecycle of a ticket while improving operational visibility, consistency, and accountability.

The application shall support different field operation roles through role-based experiences while maintaining a common interaction model.
