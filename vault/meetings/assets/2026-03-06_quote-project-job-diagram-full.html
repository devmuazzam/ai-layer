Quote / Project Rule

A Quote is created for one Customer only. The system automatically generates a Project for each approved Quote — the Project is the operational container linking back to that Quote.

Job Date Range Rule

Each Job covers a consecutive date range. If work within a Project is non-consecutive (e.g., two separate mobilizations weeks apart), each period becomes its own separate Job under the same Project.

Unit & Employee Flexibility

Units may be added to a Job without an Employee assigned. Employees may also be assigned directly to a Job independent of any Unit — covering labor-only or support roles.
