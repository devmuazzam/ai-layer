# Sprint (8) Report — TCG

**Sprint Dates**: July 27, 2026 – Aug 7th, 2026

**Duration**: 2-week sprint.

**Sprint Goal:**

Deliver the foundational back end for approximately 30% of the Atom Mobile App, while resolving
all identified bugs and implementing enhancements across the Quotation, Customer, Yard
Operations, and other developed modules.

Field worker App - Functionalities In scope

* A ticket is created in DAS and assigned to an employee.
* The employee logs into the mobile application and views their assigned tickets.
* The employee completes all required ticket steps within the app and submits the ticket.
* The complete ticket assignment and completion workflow will be supported through the mobile application.

List of Items to do : 83 work items
