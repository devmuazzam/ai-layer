## Known defects in the drop

- **`3.8.1_MODULE-ACCOUNTING-AP-AR.zip` is truncated.** 1.17 GB on disk, but the archive holds
  only two entries — `__Accounting activities.txt` and a partially-written 794 MB
  `Ariba Invoice Submitting Video.MOV`. There is no central directory; the stream ends mid-file.
  Everything else the folder was supposed to contain never arrived. **Re-request this archive.**
- **13 PDFs had no text layer** (scans/photographs), and 26 image files were not convertible to
  text. On the first pass nothing was extracted from them. **On 2026-09-04 a second pass OCR'd
  every scan (tesseract) and read every image visually** — see *The OCR and image pass* below.
  Per-group rows that still say `no text layer` or `not converted` are superseded by that section.
- **`3.7.1_MODULE-HR-LABOR-PAYROLL/` is the OneDrive 2026-08-11 payroll drop re-shared.** It was
  migrated on 2026-08-13 and is already accounted for file-by-file in
  [payroll/source-manifest.md](../payroll/source-manifest.md) and
  [payroll/sources/](../payroll/sources/INDEX.md). Not re-migrated here.
- **Portal credentials appear in the city-permit documents.** Usernames, passwords and security
  answers for city permitting portals are written in plain text in the per-city `.docx` files.
  They are **deliberately not copied into this vault**; the vault records the process and points
  back at the source file.

repeated per crane tonnage per city. What happened to it, in full:
