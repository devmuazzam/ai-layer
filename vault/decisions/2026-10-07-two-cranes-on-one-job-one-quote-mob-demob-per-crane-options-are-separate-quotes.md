# Two cranes on one job, and options or phases

## Decision

Joe Schacatano, 2026-10-07:

1. **Two main cranes on the same job go on one quote, each with its own Mobilization and
   Demobilization** — *"separate but on the same quote"* (38:29–40:32). Not one shared Mob/Demob:
   *"If we have two separate assets doing a job and they're both mobilizing, we would want to keep
   those separate"* (45:24). The quote is clearer — *"for the 100 ton, it's this much on your
   mobilization. For the 350, it's this much"* — and nothing has to be re-split if the cranes end
   up going on different days (41:22–41:55, 43:57).
2. **Each pair is labelled with its crane**, e.g. *"100 Ton Crane – Mobilization"* /
   *"100 Ton Crane – Demobilization"*. ehtesham.anwar proposed it (42:13); Joe: *"Yeah, I mean, you
   could do that."* (42:33). The label form was confirmed by syed haris (ticket owner, TCG-220) in a
   Claude Code session on 2026-10-08. Asked whether to send Joe *"With two cranes on one quote, is it
   OK to name each pair after its crane? For example, '100 Ton Crane – Mobilization' and '350 Ton
   Crane – Mobilization'"*, he replied verbatim *"2. it's decided it's okay"*: Joe's 42:33 had
   already decided it, so the question was not sent. *(Client-permitted; label wording ticket owner.)*
3. **One invoice for the job** (40:06): *"It should be one invoice… for the same job."* Separate
   invoices only if one machine stays longer than the other, or the customer asks.
4. **A supporting crane is not a second main crane** — it *"is associated in the mob and demob"*
   (43:10). See
   [2026-10-08-equipment-quoted-only-inside-mob-demob-goes-out-on-visit-days.md](2026-10-08-equipment-quoted-only-inside-mob-demob-goes-out-on-visit-days.md).
5. **Alternative options and phases are separate quotes**, under one project (45:51–48:21):
   *"moving forward… we're going to tell them they can't do it within the same quote… They're gonna
   have to be separate quotes."* Phase one is a separate job from phase two. A project can hold
   several quotes and aggregate their numbers (ehtesham.anwar 47:42; Joe: *"Perfect"*).

## Why

Joe: lumping options into one quote causes confusion and *"a lot of pending quotes just sitting
there"*; separate per-crane mob/demob keeps the customer quote readable and survives the cranes
going on different days.
