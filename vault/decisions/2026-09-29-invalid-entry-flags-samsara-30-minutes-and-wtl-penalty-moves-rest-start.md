# Invalid entries, the Samsara 30-minute flag, and the WTL penalty moves the rest start

**Why this is a third note and not an edit of the second.** It is a separate written statement.
Clause 3 is a pay rule, and it has its own source: a claimed union business agent confirmation. The
second note
([2026-09-29-flagged-entries-payroll-corrects-per-employee-and-rest-from-rounded-paid-time.md](2026-09-29-flagged-entries-payroll-corrects-per-employee-and-rest-from-rounded-paid-time.md))
keeps its C1 conflict as the record of what was contested. Its frontmatter points here.

## The exchange

Syed Haris asked, verbatim:

> "1. What makes a time entry or long-boom entry invalid? (e.g. times out of order, boom time outside
> work time)
> 2. For Samsara, how far off can the times be before we flag it? (e.g. 15 or 30 min)
> 3. On the 8-hour rest: Brian mentioned earlier that the lunch penalty shouldn't move when the rest
> starts. Could you two confirm which way it should work?"

Corrina Bustos, Teams, 2026-09-29, verbatim:

> "3. yes, the WTL penalty effect the rest starts. this was confirmed last friday in our meeting with
> the union business agent.
> 2. flagged when its further than 30 minutes outside of geotag time.
> 1.incorrect use of AM/PM. boom time same time (ex: start work 1am, end work 1am. we see that
> often), times out of order, etc"

## Decision

1. **Invalid time and long-boom entries include:**
   - incorrect AM/PM;
   - a boom or work time whose start and end are the same. Her example: *"start work 1am, end work
     1am. we see that often"*. She wrote it under "boom time" but her example names Start/End Work,
     so it is recorded as covering both;
   - times out of order.

   The list ends *"etc"*. **It is not exhaustive.** The one example Syed Haris offered that she did
   not repeat, *boom time outside work time*, is neither confirmed nor rejected.
2. **Samsara flag tolerance: 30 minutes.** An entry is flagged when its time is *"further than 30
   minutes outside of geotag time"*, meaning more than 30 minutes from the Samsara geotag time.
3. **The WTL penalty moves the rest start.** *"yes, the WTL penalty effect the rest starts"*. This
   confirms clause 5 of the second note: the gap runs from the rounded paid end, including the WTL
   penalty. Her stated source is the union business agent meeting on 2026-09-25.
