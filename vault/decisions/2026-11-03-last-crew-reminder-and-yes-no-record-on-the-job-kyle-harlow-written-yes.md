# Last-crew entitlement: a reminder to call first, and a yes/no record on the job

*`verified` here means that Kyle Harlow gave this Yes. It does not mean the rule has been checked
against the union agreement (see* Conflicts and interactions*), and it does not extend to Syed Haris's
direction setting Melissa McNutt's examples aside, which is labelled as his throughout.*

## Decision

Kyle Harlow agreed to this form, which goes further than the *"in the system"* of
[2026-04-09-last-crew-entitlement-managed-in-the-system-offers-and-refusals-recorded.md](2026-04-09-last-crew-entitlement-managed-in-the-system-offers-and-refusals-recorded.md):

1. **When dispatch puts that crane on the next shift, the system shows who ran it last, with a
   reminder to call them first.**
2. **Dispatch marks who said yes and who said no, and that stays on the job as a record.**

The trigger is Kyle's rule, which he confirmed in writing:

3. If the crew finished their shift on overtime and the same crane is going out again **before
   6 AM**, those crew members should be called first. If the crane goes out **at 6 or later**,
   dispatch can put anyone on it.

**Melissa McNutt's examples are set aside by Syed Haris's direction (2026-11-03) on who owns this
rule.** Her e-mail of 2026-04-08 ([../erp-review-log.md](../erp-review-log.md) #4, `unverified`) has a
crew on straight time, 06:00–11:00, entitled to a 19:00 night job on the same crane, and Friday
carrying to Saturday. His direction: Kyle is the right person to answer the rule, Melissa handles
payroll, and her e-mail's trigger is not a competing answer. Neither Kyle nor Melissa overruled
anything: Kyle was not shown her case, and she was not asked.

## The exchange — verbatim

Syed Haris to Kyle Harlow:

> Hi Kyle Harlow, as discussed about offering the next shift to the crane's last crew.
> From what I understood, if the crew finished their shift on overtime and the same crane is going out again before 6 AM, those crew members should be called first. If the crane goes out at 6 or later, dispatch can put anyone on it.
> My plan is that when dispatch puts that crane on the next shift, the system shows who ran it last with a reminder to call them first. Dispatch then marks who said yes and who said no, and that stays on the job as a record.
> Does that sound right to you?

Kyle Harlow:

> Yes, that should work fine thank you.

## What the Yes covers

It is one line, answering *"Does that sound right to you?"* about a whole message. Kyle restated
nothing and added nothing, so it covers the message's words and no more:

- **The form is a reminder.** The message does not describe a block, a required action before
  saving, or a ranking. It also does not rule them out, because they were not put to him.
- **The message states when to call first, but never says "only".** The *"only"* is Kyle's spoken
  statement on 2026-10-22 (*"It's only if they ended on an overtime"*,
  [../meetings/transcripts/2026-10-22-erp-demo-walkthrough-part-2.md](../meetings/transcripts/2026-10-22-erp-demo-walkthrough-part-2.md),
  1:09:32). The Yes itself says nothing about a crew that did not finish on overtime and a crane
  going out before 6 AM. Together with Syed Haris's direction, that spoken *"only"* is what removes
  a crew that did not finish on overtime.
- **The AM is confirmed for the "before" limb.** The transcript reads *"6 P.m."* at 1:08:40 and
  *"6 A.m."* at 1:08:58; the message says *"before 6 AM"*. The *"at 6 or later"* limb carries no AM
  or PM, in the message or in the transcript.
- **The restatement is narrower than what Kyle said on 2026-10-22, and his Yes does not withdraw the
  rest.** Asked *"Overtime for them, or overtime as a general?"*, he answered *"for the machine"*
  (1:08:45–1:08:58). The message says the *crew* finished on overtime. He also said *"in the same
  shift"* and that it needs refining (*"we'll probably have to like refine that"*, 1:09:32;
  *"something that we need to refine"*, 1:09:58). The message mentions none of these.
- **The record is on "the job".** Which entity in the ERP that means is not stated.

## Why

The message gives no reason and neither does Kyle's reply. The reasons on record are the ones from
2026-04-09: the judgement changes with whoever is dispatching, a miss is expensive, and a recorded
refusal is evidence that the crew was offered the work. See the 2026-04-09 note. Nothing here adds
to them.

## What was not decided

Item letters are those of the 2026-04-09 note and of
[../dispatch/gaps.md](../dispatch/gaps.md) → *First right of refusal on the next day's crane —
last-crew entitlement*, which **stays open**.

- **(a) One wording inside Kyle's rule.** The trigger is settled on Kyle's rule (see *Decision*), but
  whether *overtime* means the crew's or the machine's is not: he said *"for the machine"* on
  2026-10-22, and the message he agreed to says the crew.
- **(b) What counts as the next-shift overtime.** *"Before 6 AM"* and *"at 6 or later"* are the only
  words given. Open: a same-evening or same-day restart (a crew ends overtime at 6 PM and the crane
  goes out at 8 PM), and weekends. Setting aside Melissa's 19:00 and Friday-to-Saturday examples does
  not answer these for a crew that did finish on overtime.
- **(c) Who holds the right.** *"Crew members"* and *"who ran it last"* do not say whether it extends
  to a driver or a rigger.
- **(d) The pay owed when the crew is passed over. Not mentioned, and this note states no pay rule
  (G4).** Nothing goes into [../payroll/payroll-rules-provenance.md](../payroll/payroll-rules-provenance.md).
- **(e) When the reminder appears.** The plan's sentence puts no condition on it. Whether it shows
  only when item 3's conditions hold, or every time that crane goes on a next shift, is not stated.
- **(f) The rest of the record.** Open: time of offer, channel, which dispatcher (*"dispatch"* does
  not say the dispatch manager or any dispatcher), what happens if the crew does not answer, and what
  happens if dispatch assigns someone else without marking anything. Lisette Anzaldo, whom the gap
  names as owner for how the offer is recorded, was not part of this exchange.
- **How the crew is reached.** The plan is a reminder to *call*. It does not decide against texting
  or system replies ([../erp-review-log.md](../erp-review-log.md) #1; 2026-04-09 note, last item).
