# Truck Crane MLA rate sheet — effective 2026-07-01

**What this is.** The union's own six-page rate sheet for the Truck Crane Master Labor
Agreement, headed *Effective July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 · Revised June 2026 · Supersedes
August 2025*. It is the document that fills in the two wage columns the 2025-08-15 notice left
blank ([local-12-wage-notice-2025-08-15.md](local-12-wage-notice-2025-08-15.md)), and it
restates the working rules the contract spreads over several articles on one page. Two copies
were in the drop; the one named `incorrectOldVersion` differs in **exactly one number** — the
Vacation-Holiday Supplemental Dues column, $3.45 instead of $1.75, which moves Total Fringes
from $39.50 to $41.20 and every Total Package by $1.70. Wages, apprentice percentages,
classifications and rules are identical. Use the *corrected* figures below.

**What it settles.** The 2026-07-01 wage allocation is **$1.00 to wages on every group**
(the $5.00 total-package increase, of which $4.00 went to fringes). The ADP workbook's FY26-27
column ([../adp-workbook-power-query-engine.md](../adp-workbook-power-query-engine.md) §5) and
FCC's `FMS_UNION_CRAFT` rates on 2026-07-01
([../fcc-employee-master-reference-data.md](../fcc-employee-master-reference-data.md) §5) both
carry exactly these figures — they were keyed from this sheet, not assumed.

\* **Lattice boom**: $1.00 per hour over the wage rate for the operator, 50¢ for the oiler, **Group
6 and above** only. **Hazardous material** (suit-up, Level A / B regulated work): $2.00 per hour,
and that rate becomes the basic hourly rate; employees on such work shall not work alone.

## The rules the sheet restates

| Rule | Text on the sheet |
| --- | --- |
| **Long boom** | 80 ft up to 100 ft, including jib: 75¢/h for the operator; a further 75¢/h for each additional 20 ft or part thereof. The added rate is the **base rate for overtime**. Does not apply to travel and curfew hours yard↔job. When the operator gets the 75¢, the oiler on a high-boom crane gets **25¢/h**. Telescopic booms capable of 80 ft or more: long-boom pay for time actually worked at the higher rate, **minimum 4 h per job worked with a long boom, not to exceed total time worked** |
| **Mobile tower crane** | Crew of two journeyman operators; long-boom rate on mast + luffing boom combined; erecting/dismantling needs the crew plus two additional covered employees unless automatic |
| **Tools** | Employer replaces employee-furnished tools worn out or broken on the job, on evidence; tool insurance provided; employee submits an inventory |
| **Work day / week** | Eight consecutive hours between 6:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m.; 40 hours Monday–Friday at straight time |
| **Overtime** | Not less than 1½× outside regular hours; **2× after 12 hours, all Sundays and holidays**; no pyramiding |
| **Special rules** | The regularly assigned operator works the before/after-shift and weekend overtime on that equipment, except emergencies or when the employer judges >16 h without an 8 h break a safety issue — then the relief employee is paid at the relieved employee's rate, and the original employee gets the job back on a further change |
| **Multiple shifts** | Three or more consecutive days, union notified in advance; shifts may not overlap and the interval between them may not exceed one hour. 1st shift 8 h for 8 h pay; **2nd shift 7 h for 8 h pay plus $3.00/h; 3rd shift 6½ h for 8 h pay plus $4.00/h** — the premium becomes the base rate for the whole shift; the short-hours-for-eight rule also applies on Saturday, Sunday and holidays at overtime rates |
| **Subsistence** | **$85.00 per scheduled work day** when required to remain away from home overnight; **$200.00 per day** in a subsistence area over 77½ miles from home base, with the employer's option for weekends to pay subsistence or portal yard↔job at the wage rate |
| **Increases** | Past: 7/1/23 +$4.00, 7/1/24 +$4.00, 7/1/25 +$6.00, **7/1/26 +$5.00** total package. Future: **7/1/27 +$4.50** total package |

## 2026 Local 12 holiday bulletin (page 6)

Eight hours minimum at **3×** for all work on a holiday. A holiday on Sunday moves to Monday; Christmas
or New Year's Day on Saturday moves to the preceding Friday. The first Saturday after the first Friday
of June and of December is paid at **2×** if worked.
