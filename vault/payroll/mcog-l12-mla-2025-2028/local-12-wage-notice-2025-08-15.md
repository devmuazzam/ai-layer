# Local 12 wage and fringe notice to Short Form Truck Crane contractors, 2025-08-15

**What this is.** A four-page letter on IUOE Local 12 letterhead, dated **August 15, 2025**, from
**David K. Sikorski, Business Manager and General Vice President**, countersigned by **Perry
Hawkins III, Financial Secretary**, addressed *"TO: ALL CONTRACTORS SIGNATORY TO THE SHORT FORM
TRUCK CRANE AGREEMENT"*, *"RE: TRUCK CRANE MASTER LABOR AGREEMENT/2025-2028"*. It publishes the
wage rates and fringe contributions agreed in the 2025 negotiations and asks contractors to
*"place these new rates into effect as of July 1, 2025."* The file's name (`07.01.2025 truck
crane MLA`) is misleading — this is the **rate notice**, not the agreement.

The scan had no text layer. It was OCR'd and then **read page by page against the image** on
2026-09-04; the figures below are transcribed from the image, not from the OCR. This is the
cleanest statement of the 2025-07-01 rates anywhere in the vault.

## Page 1 — allocation of the negotiated increase

| Effective | Negotiated increase |
| --- | ---: |
| 7-01-25 | **$6.00** |
| 7-01-26 | **$5.00** |
| 7-01-27 | **$4.50** |

The $6.00 effective July 1 2025 is allocated: **$5.00 to hourly wage rates** (page 2) and
**$1.00 to Health and Welfare**, taking it to **$14.20** for all hours worked or paid.

The asterisk on each increase: *"The Union may elect at its option, upon at least sixty (60)
days' written notice to allocate an increase indicated to: (1) Hourly Wage Rates, (2) Health and
Welfare, (3) Pension, (4) Vacation-Holiday and/or Supplemental Dues, (5) Joint Apprenticeship and
Journeyman Retraining Trust, (6) P.A.C. and (7) Defined Contribution Plan (Annuity)."* So the
2026 and 2027 splits between wages and fringes are **not yet fixed** by this letter.

| 2 | Truck Crane / Crawler Crane **Oiler** (over 80 tons) | $61.18 | **$66.18** |

## Page 3 — apprentices, subsistence, premiums

**Apprentice wage rates**, based on the **Group 5** rate of $68.16:

| Hours | Step | % of Group 5 |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 0–1000 | I | 60% |
| 1000–2000 | II | 65% |
| 2000–3000 | III | 70% |
| 3000–4000 | IV | 75% |
| 4000–5000 | V | 80% |
| 5000–6000 | VI | 90% |

*"At no time shall the above Apprentice wage rates exceed any of the Journeyman Group Rates of
this Agreement."*

**Subsistence.** $85.00 per scheduled work day (since 2007-08-15). Since **2022-07-01, $200.00
per scheduled work day (seven days a week)** if the job is located **more than 77½ miles** from
the employer's home base.

**Additional premiums:**

- Second shift **+$3.00/hr**, third shift **+$4.00/hr** (since 2007-08-15).
- **Double time after 12 hours worked** (since 2007-08-15).
- Double time continues **until the Article III §E rest-period requirement is met**.
- **Lattice boom**, Group 6 and above: **+$1.00/hr operator, +$0.50/hr oiler**.
- **Long Boom Pay** increased from $0.50 to **$0.75** (since 2001-07-01).
- **Hazardous-material** suit-up work: **+$2.00/hr**, and that rate becomes the basic hourly rate.

## What this settles, and what it does not

- The **$0.75 Long Boom rate** and the **$1.00 / $0.50 lattice-boom adders** are stated here as
  flat figures; the vault's rule register carries long-boom pay as a bracketed adder by boom
  length ([../rules/](../rules/INDEX.md), `PAY-043`). This letter does not describe brackets. Which
  is the operative reading is a question for payroll and the union, not for this page.
- The **77½-mile subsistence threshold and $200/day** corroborate
  [03-article-v-subsistence-allowance.md](03-article-v-subsistence-allowance.md).
- The 2026 and 2027 wage columns are **blank by design** — do not cite a 2026 rate from this
  document.
