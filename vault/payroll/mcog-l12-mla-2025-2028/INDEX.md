# MCOG / OE Local 12 Truck Crane Master Labor Agreement, 2025–2028

**The contract every L12 payroll rule ultimately derives from.** Effective July 1 2025 through
June 30 2028. This is an extract: the source document opens with *"The following are ONLY
sections/information related to payroll"*, so non-payroll articles were removed before it reached
us.

> **This is contract text, not an interpretation of it.** Where a payroll rule and this document
> disagree, the disagreement is a question for TCG management, the payroll team, labor counsel and
> the union — it is not something to resolve by reading harder. Both the ERP requirements document
> and the rule matrix say so explicitly and neither claims to be legal advice.

## The clauses that drive the calculation

Anchors for the rules implemented downstream — quoted in full in part 01:

| Clause | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Eight-hour day / window | 8 consecutive hours between **06:00 and 17:00**; 40 hours Mon–Fri at straight time. Overtime outside regular hours ≥ 1½×; **double time after 12 hours**, all Sundays and holidays. **"There shall be no pyramiding of overtime."** |
| Multiple shifts | 1st: 8 worked / 8 paid. 2nd: 7 worked / 8 paid, **+$3.00/hr**. 3rd: 6½ worked / 8 paid, **+$4.00/hr**. The premium **"will become the base rate for the entire shift"**. Requires 3+ consecutive days and advance written notice to the Union |
| Hazmat | **+$2.00/hr**, becomes the basic hourly rate; **Level "A" and Level "B" regulated work only**; employee shall not work alone |
| Holidays | **Minimum 8 hours at triple time** on the nine listed holidays. Sunday holiday → Monday observed. Christmas/New Year on Saturday → Friday preceding. **No work on Labor Day except extreme urgency** |
| Special Saturday | First Saturday following the first Friday in **June and December**: minimum 8 hours at **double time**. Non-construction work may be worked at double time by Union permit |
| Craft pay rate | Employees receive the **same pay as the craft they work with**, if more advantageous — including that craft's overtime and subsistence provisions |
| Rounding | **"All time, including overtime, shall be reckoned on the one-half (½) hour."** |
| Rest periods | **Not less than 8 hours** between the end of an overtime period and the start of another straight-time shift. If not received, **applicable overtime rate for each hour worked until 8 hours rest is received off the job** |
| Meal periods | No more than **5 consecutive hours** without a ½-hour meal period. Worked meal periods paid at **double time**, *in addition to* minimum pay requirements. On Saturdays/Sundays/holidays, an **additional ½ hour at the applicable wage rate** per missed meal |
| Reporting, no work | **2 hours** pay — unless notified before the end of the last shift, or ≥2 hours before start time, or a written inclement-weather policy applies |
| Work provided | **Not less than 4 hours** pay. Over 4 hours worked → paid actual time at the applicable rate |
| Crane remains on job | On days after the first: **8 hours** minimum — unless operations cease for rain/wind or mechanical/electronic malfunction and the crew is released, then **4 hours**; **>4 worked → 6 hours**; **>6 worked → 8 hours** |
| First-day exception | **No 8-hour guarantee if only traveling, assembling or preparing the crane**, first day only. If customer work is performed and hours < 8, the employee must call the dispatcher to qualify for the guarantee |
| Not qualified / unable | An employee who reports but is not qualified, or cannot work due to their own condition, **receives no reporting time** |
