# PAY-033 — Holiday pay

**Rule:** Pay actual and guaranteed hours at applicable holiday rate.

**Applies to:** Eligible L12 labor. **Trigger:** Work occurs on configured holiday.
**Requires:** Hours; rate; holiday multiplier.
