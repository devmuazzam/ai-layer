# PAY-016 — Apprentice wage step

**Rule:** Apply effective apprentice percentage and flag step advancement.

**Applies to:** L12 apprentices. **Trigger:** Employee reaches configured accumulated-hour threshold.
**Requires:** Apprentice status; hours; step; journeyman group.
