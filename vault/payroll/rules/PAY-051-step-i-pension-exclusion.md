# PAY-051 — Step I pension exclusion

**Rule:** Exclude pension contribution when agreement criteria apply.

**Applies to:** Qualifying apprentices. **Trigger:** Apprentice is within first qualifying 1,000 hours.
**Requires:** Indenture date; cumulative hours.
