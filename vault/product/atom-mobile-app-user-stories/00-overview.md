## TCG : Atom Field Mobile Application

The **Atom Mobile Field Application** supports TCG field employees throughout the complete job
lifecycle, from ticket assignment and inspections to job completion.

It captures operational, safety, customer, equipment, compliance, and labour-related information
directly from the field.

### Field Workflow summary :

Login → Dashboard → Assigned Ticket → Ticket Details → Pre-Trip Inspection → Left Yard → Travel
to Job → Arrive Job & Customer Authorization → JSA/Tailboard → Work Execution → Lunch/WTL as
applicable → Customer Final Signature → Left Job & Return Travel → Arrive Yard → Post-Trip
Inspection → Compliance → Labor & Unit Details → Review & Complete Ticket
