## 3. Objectives

The Field Operations mobile experience shall aim to:

* Standardize ticket execution processes across field employees.
* Improve usability for field personnel.
* Increase compliance and auditability.
* Improve operational transparency.
* Reduce manual reconciliation efforts.
* Improve the quality and completeness of field records.
* Support future payroll automation initiatives.
* Establish a scalable foundation for future field operations enhancements.
