## 6. Operational Workflow Overview

The standard ticket lifecycle shall follow the sequence below.

```
Ticket Assigned
↓
Ticket Accepted (if required)
↓
Pre-Trip Checklist
↓
Leave Yard
↓
Arrive Job Site
↓
Job Safety Analysis (JSA)
↓
Start Work
↓
Lunch / Work Through Lunch
↓
End Work
↓
Compliance Checklist
↓
Leave Job Site
↓
Arrive Yard
↓
Post-Trip Checklist
↓
Customer Signature
↓
Submit Ticket
```

The workflow may vary based on role responsibilities, ticket type, and configurable business rules.
