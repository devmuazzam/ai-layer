## 2. Problem Statement

Current field operations require employees to rely on multiple systems and manual processes to complete a single job.

The existing process introduces challenges including:

* Limited visibility into ticket progress.
* Multiple disconnected workflows.
* Manual reconciliation of labor information.
* Inconsistent handling of inspections and deficiencies.
* Difficulty tracking support load activities.
* Limited operational visibility into field execution.
* Increased payroll disputes caused by incomplete or inconsistent records.
* Reduced efficiency caused by repetitive data entry and fragmented user experiences.

The future solution shall consolidate these activities into a single mobile experience.
