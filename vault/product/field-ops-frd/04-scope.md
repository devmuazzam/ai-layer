## 4. Scope

### Included

This FRD covers the Field Operations experience within the TCG Mobile Application.

Included capabilities:

* Ticket processing.
* Job execution workflows.
* Ticket lifecycle activities.
* Pre-trip inspections.
* Post-trip inspections.
* Job Safety Analysis (JSA).
* Lunch workflows.
* Work-through lunch workflows.
* Compliance activities.
* Labor visibility.
* Customer signatures.
* Deficiency reporting.
* Downtime tracking.
* Notifications.
* Certifications.
* Field tasks.
* Inspection history.

### Excluded

The following capabilities are outside the scope of this FRD:

* Dispatch planning workflows.
* Sales workflows.
* Payroll calculations.
* ADP processing logic.
* Union calculation logic.
* Accounting workflows.
* Customer portal experiences.
* Asset maintenance management workflows.
* Yard personnel workflows.
* Mechanic workflows.
