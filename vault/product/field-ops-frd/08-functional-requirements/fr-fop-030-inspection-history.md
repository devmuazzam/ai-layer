### FR-FOP-030 — Inspection History

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| ID | FR-FOP-030 |
| Module | Inspections |
| Title | Inspection History |
| Description | The Application shall support visibility into historical inspection activities completed by field employees. Inspection History provides operational awareness and supports regulatory inquiries. |
| Functional requirements | The Application shall display historical inspection records and support viewing Pre-Trip and Post-Trip inspection history. The Application shall support inspection search and filtering capabilities. The Application shall support viewing inspection details, associated deficiencies, and associated photos. The Application shall preserve inspection audit information. |
| Business rules | Inspection visibility shall respect user permissions. Historical retention requirements shall be defined within the BRS. Inspection History requirements shall support operational and regulatory needs. Inspection activities shall remain auditable. |
| Acceptance criteria | **AC-001** Given inspection records exist, When users review Inspection History, Then historical inspection information shall be displayed. **AC-002** Given search criteria are provided, When users perform inspection searches, Then matching inspection records shall be displayed. **AC-003** Given inspection deficiencies exist, When inspection details are reviewed, Then associated deficiency information shall be displayed. |
