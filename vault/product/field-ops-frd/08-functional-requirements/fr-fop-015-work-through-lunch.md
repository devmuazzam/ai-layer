### FR-FOP-015 — Work Through Lunch

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| ID | FR-FOP-015 |
| Module | Lunch |
| Title | Work Through Lunch |
| Description | The Application shall support circumstances where employees continue working instead of taking required lunch periods. |
| Functional requirements | The Application shall support Work Through Lunch requests and authorizations. The Application shall support customer signature collection and employee signatures where applicable. The Application shall support comments and preserve audit information. The Application shall support multiple Work Through Lunch occurrences. The Application shall maintain visibility into Work Through Lunch status. The Application shall support N/A states where lunch obligations do not apply. |
| Business rules | Work Through Lunch requirements shall be configurable. Work Through Lunch activities shall contribute to compliance evaluations. Detailed Work Through Lunch rules shall be defined within the BRS. |
| Acceptance criteria | **AC-001** Given a lunch obligation exists, When the employee continues working, Then Work Through Lunch workflows shall be available. **AC-002** Given Work Through Lunch is completed, When compliance evaluations occur, Then Work Through Lunch status shall be considered. |
