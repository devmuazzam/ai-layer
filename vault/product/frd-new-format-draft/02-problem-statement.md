## 2. Problem Statement

Field employees currently manage job execution through a combination of paper-based forms, phone calls to dispatch, and manual time entry. This creates several operational problems:

* Ticket paperwork is completed informally, leading to missing signatures, incorrect times, and payroll disputes.
* There is no structured lunch tracking — compliance confirmations are written manually or skipped entirely.
* Pre-trip and compliance checklists are paper-based with no deficiency escalation path.
* Labor details are entered after the fact from memory, not from captured timestamps.
* Incomplete tickets are not surfaced or enforced — payroll and billing are delayed as a result.
* Boom pay and craft modifier selection is inconsistently applied across crews.

The TCG Mobile Application encodes the full field operations workflow into a guided, auditable, role-aware mobile experience — so every ticket is completed correctly the first time, and payroll and billing receive clean, timestamped data automatically.
