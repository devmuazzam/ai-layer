## 3. Feature Scope

* Role-based bottom navigation and home dashboard oriented around current operational work
* Full guided ticket lifecycle: Leave Yard → Pre-Trip → Arrive Job → JSA → Start Work → Lunch Handling → End Work → Final Signature → Leave Job → Arrive Yard → Compliance → Labor → Submit
* Downtime and delay capture with classification and conditioned customer acknowledgement
* Customer signature capture — initial and final — with configurable photo evidence
* Compliance checklist with per diem, lunch, and rest confirmations
* Labor detail entry with underlying timestamp display and boom pay / craft modifier capture
* Configurable incomplete ticket enforcement — warning, soft-block, and hard-block tiers
* Deficiency capture with three formal severity tiers: Normal, Urgent, Blocking
* Certification management with expiry alerts and renewal upload
* Notification center covering all operational alerts
* Field task list with status tracking
* Labor history with payroll modification visibility
* Emergency phonebook with one-tap calling
* Cross-cutting audit trail, auto-save, and time rounding across all ticket events
