## 1. Overview

This document defines the functional requirements for the TCG Mobile Application — specifically the Field Operations role group. It covers the complete ticket lifecycle executed by field employees from the moment they leave the yard to final ticket submission, along with all supporting modules: certifications, compliance, labor, notifications, tasks, and the emergency phonebook.

The application is role-driven. All navigation, workflow steps, and data visibility are scoped to the user's assigned role. The ticket lifecycle is the dominant flow. All other modules exist to support or extend it.

This version (v1.1) incorporates Revision Pass 1 changes from transcript alignment: six existing requirements have been strengthened, two new requirements have been added, and three open questions from v1.0 have been resolved.
