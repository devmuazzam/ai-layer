# Engagement directory — who is who at TCG and CodingCops, and the TCG org charts

**What this is.** The ERP project's contact list, plus what can be read from the three TCG
organisation charts in the drop. The vault has many documents attributed to first names — Melissa,
Corrina, Wes, Brian, Joe S., Lisette — and until now nothing that says who they are. This page
resolves those names to roles.

> **Names and work roles only.** Email addresses in the source are work addresses at
> `craneguys.com` and `codingcops.com`; they are reproduced here because they are the routing
> information the project needs. **No personal contact details, home addresses, phone numbers or
> employee records appear in the source and none are recorded here.**

**What changed between September 2025 and June 2026** (the two charts, read side by side):

- **Dispatch moved under Sales.** In 2025 the Dispatch Manager sat in the manager tier under the
  VP of Operations; in 2026 Dispatch Manager and Sales Manager both report to the **VP of Sales**.
  That is the organisational form of the sell-side → dispatch hand-off the sales playbooks
  describe.
- **Joe Schacatano's title changed** from VP of Operations to **VP of Sales**; **Kyle Harlow's**
  from VP of Fleet to **VP of Fleet and Field Operations**.
- **Three roles appear that the 2025 chart did not have:** CFO (Brent Wagstaff, above the
  Controller), Safety Manager (Dennene Jones, replacing the unnamed "Managing Partners" safety box),
  and Data Analytics (Brian Petros). **Certified Payroll** (Rose Aguilera) is now a named box under HR.
- **Roles on the 2025 chart that have no box in 2026:** Project Engineer Lead (Leo Lopez — now on
  the Sales Team), Field Operations / Project Manager (Joe Deitrick — now on the Sales Team), Sales
  Team Lead (Jason Flores — now a Sales Team member), Compliance Specialist, Parts Purchasing,
  Field Supervisor. Dylan Masterson moved from Shop Foreman to **Facility Manager**; the shop is now
  under **Elliott Gonzalez** as Shop Manager.
- **IT has no box on either chart.** Patrick McKiernan (IT Manager on the contact list) does not
  appear; nor does any IT function.

This resolves the question the earlier version of this page left open: the June 2026 chart **is**
the current picture, it **does** reconcile with the ERP contact list (Wagstaff, Jones, Adams and
Petros are all on it), and the 2025 chart is superseded. The remaining oddity is the *"TCG-TRG"*
prefix on one filename — the chart itself says only "The Crane Guys, LLC" and names no second
entity.

> **A note for dispatch.** The vault's dispatch pages record that one 2026-04-29 value-engine
> chart names *"Garret"* as dispatch owner. The only Garrett on this chart is **Garrett Harlow, in
> Mechanics and Parts**. The org chart does not support a dispatch owner by that name.
