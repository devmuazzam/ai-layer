# Schedule changes, reschedules and cancellations — including after hours

**What this is.** Two playbooks. The **Schedule Change** playbook (Version 1.0) governs any
customer-requested change to a scheduled job during business hours. The **After-Hours Schedule
Change / Reschedule / Cancellation** playbook (Version 1.0) governs the same requests received
overnight, and hands them back to the Schedule Change playbook the next morning. Both are
"Executive/Operations Playbooks" in TCG's Value Engine series.

## Schedule change — the standard rule

> **No schedule change is final until** the operational impact has been reviewed, all affected
> departments have been notified, any additional charges have been approved by the customer, and
> **Dispatch has received the updated scheduling information.**

**Trigger:** a customer requests a change to a scheduled job's date, time, location, scope, crane,
or any other detail that may affect planning or execution.

## What a schedule change costs

The playbook's cost-impact table — six items that must be reviewed on **every** change:

| Item | Review required |
| --- | --- |
| **Permit revisions or reissuance** | May require additional permit fees |
| **Lift plan revisions** | **Engineering time may be billable** |
| **Traffic control / lane closures** | Vendor rescheduling or cancellation fees |
| **Pilot cars / escorts** | Additional vendor charges may apply |
| **Third-party vendors** | Confirm change fees or cancellation costs |
| **Mobilization / demobilization** | Review equipment and labor impacts |

## Who does what

**Sales Coordinator:** document the requested change · review all operational and billing impacts ·
**advise the customer of any additional charges before proceeding** · obtain customer approval ·
update the Scheduling Sheet and supporting documents · **notify Dispatch immediately**.

**Dispatch:** review the revised schedule · confirm crane and crew availability · coordinate with
affected departments · update the dispatch schedule **after approval**.

## The workflow

1. Receive the customer's request.
2. Determine what changed — date, time, crane, location, scope, etc.
3. Review impacts to permits, lift plans and third-party vendors.
4. Calculate any additional costs.
5. Explain the costs to the customer and **obtain approval**.
6. Update all scheduling documents.
7. Send the revised package to Dispatch.
8. Dispatch confirms the updated schedule.

## The impact checklist

`Dispatch notified` · `Permit revisions reviewed` · `Lift plan revisions reviewed` ·
`FAA / NOTAM impacts reviewed (if applicable)` · `Traffic control reviewed` ·
`Third-party vendors contacted` · `Additional charges calculated` ·
`Customer approved additional charges` · `Updated Scheduling Sheet sent to Dispatch`

**GO** — all impacts reviewed, customer has approved any additional charges, Dispatch has the
updated job information. **NO-GO** — customer approval missing, additional costs not addressed, or
operational impacts unresolved.

**Common mistakes:** changing the schedule without notifying Dispatch · failing to review permit
impacts · **forgetting to bill for lift plan revisions** · overlooking third-party cancellation or
rescheduling fees · not documenting customer approval.

## After hours — the ownership split

**Scope:** every after-hours customer email, phone call, voicemail or text affecting a scheduled
crane, trucking, rigging, permit or support service.

The playbook's central design is an explicit ownership handover at start of business:

| Process step | Owner | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receive after-hours request | **Dispatch** | Capture immediately |
| Notify field personnel | **Dispatch** | **Prevent unnecessary mobilization** |
| Update overnight dispatch schedule | **Dispatch** | Remove or flag affected resources |
| Acknowledge customer | **Dispatch** | Confirm receipt |
| Review overnight requests | **Sales Administration** | Begin business-day ownership |
| Clarify unclear requests | Sales Administration **with** Dispatch | Resolve missing details |
| Process schedule change | **Sales Administration** | Follow the Schedule Change playbook |
| Customer follow-up | Sales Administration / Sales | Confirm the revised schedule |

### Dispatch's overnight procedure — eight steps

1. Receive the customer's request.
2. Verify customer, project, jobsite and requested action.
3. Determine whether it is a **cancellation, reschedule or schedule change**.
4. **Immediately notify all assigned operators, drivers, riggers and support personnel.**
5. Remove the crew and equipment from the active schedule, or **clearly mark the job pending**.
6. **Document the request, the time received and the person making it.**
7. Send a professional acknowledgement to the customer confirming receipt.
8. Prepare clear notes for the morning handoff.

The playbook supplies the exact customer acknowledgement wording, which confirms receipt, states
that dispatch has been notified **to prevent unnecessary mobilization**, and says Sales
Administration will make contact during normal business hours.

### Sales Administration's morning procedure

**Step 1** — review all overnight emails, voicemails, dispatch notes and customer communications.
**Step 2** — if the request is complete and clearly understood, **take ownership immediately and
proceed with the Schedule Change playbook; Dispatch does not need to be contacted.**
**Step 3** — if anything is unclear, incomplete or conflicting, **contact Dispatch to clarify what
happened overnight before making changes.**
**Step 4** — complete the Schedule Change playbook, including: update the schedule · **update
HubSpot/CRM** · **update permits if required** · notify affected departments · coordinate revised
dates with Sales · send customer confirmations · complete all required documentation.

> `HubSpot/CRM` is the only mention of a CRM system in the scheduling set. No other playbook names
> the system of record for a schedule change; the Lift Plan Submission Procedure names
> **NexgenFMS/FCC**, and the NOTAMs playbook names **iCranetrax**. The drop does not reconcile them.

### Special situations

- **Permit-required jobs** — determine whether permits must be **revised or reissued**.
- **Public Works / Davis-Bacon** — verify any owner notification or compliance impacts.
- **Same-day mobilizations** — contact Operations immediately **if equipment has already departed**.
- **Emergency utility work** — escalate to Operations and Sales immediately.

### The golden rules, verbatim

1. Safety before schedule.
2. Respond to every customer.
3. Notify crews immediately.
4. Document everything.
5. **Sales Administration owns the request the next business day.**
6. **Dispatch is contacted only if clarification is needed.**
7. Every completed request must follow the Schedule Change Playbook.

### KPIs

After-hours response time · crew notification time · **prevented unnecessary mobilizations** ·
customer acknowledgement time · overnight requests processed before start of business ·
schedule change accuracy.
