## Three facts worth carrying forward

1. **The deliverable is tiered and priced** — Ground Bearing Pressures / Standard / Premium /
   Revisions, with a P.E. stamp charged per plan on top of Premium. Ready to Schedule requires the
   lift plan on a job to be a **billable line item**; Schedule Change requires **revisions** to be
   reviewed for billable engineering time.
2. **A package bundles four categories of expiring credential** — crane certifications, operator
   certifications, rigging capacity references and the certificate of insurance. A stored package is
   only as valid as its attachments.
3. **The evaluation form may be the customer's, not TCG's** — one sample uses LAUSD's Lift Evaluation
   Form, submitted to a school-district safety mailbox.
