# **View & Manage Job Details**

**As a** Dispatcher **I want to** open a job and see all its details, resources, and tabs in one place **So that** I have everything I need to manage and execute the job without jumping between screens

**User Flow**

1. User opens a Project → clicks into a Job
2. Lands on the **Main tab** by default
3. Reviews customer info, job site, work description, quotation details, and contacts
4. Clicks the **Resources tab** to view or assign equipment and crew pulled from the quotation
5. Adds, updates, or removes resources as needed
6. Navigates to other tabs — Schedule, Progress Tracking, Tool/Rigging, Attachments
7. Saves any changes
8. Job data flows automatically into DAS and other relevant views

**Acceptance Criteria**

**General**

* Every job has a unique Job ID auto-generated by the system
* Job is always linked to a parent Project
* Job status is clearly visible at the top of the job screen
* Main tab loads by default when a job is opened

**Main Tab**

* Customer name, job site, billing type, and salesperson are displayed and pre-filled from the quotation
* Work description and notes are visible and editable
* Quotation number is visible and clickable — it links directly to the original quotation
* Contact details (Quoted To, Report To) are displayed
* Any editable field can be updated and saved without affecting the linked quotation

**Resources Tab**

* Equipment and crew that were added at the quotation level automatically pull through to this tab — no re-entry needed
* Resources are shown in a parent-child structure — e.g. a crane (parent) has an operator and oiler listed under it (children)
* Dispatcher can assign a specific unit to a resource slot (e.g. assign Unit #901 to the 100-ton crane slot)
* Dispatcher can add new resources, update existing ones, or remove them
* Resource categories (crane, truck, etc.) and subcategories are selectable when adding a new resource
* A job can have multiple resources
* If a resource is already assigned to another job at the same time, the system shows a warning

**Other Tabs**

* User can freely navigate between all tabs: Main, Resources, Schedule, Progress Tracking, Tool/Rigging, Attachments
* Each tab saves independently — navigating away does not lose unsaved work on another tab

**Data Flow**

* All job details (customer, crane type, work description) automatically carry into the Schedule and DAS — nothing needs to be re-entered
* Any update made to the job is reflected in real time across all views (DAS, Schedule tab, Dylan's loadout view)
* Anyone with the right access level can view the job and its tabs
