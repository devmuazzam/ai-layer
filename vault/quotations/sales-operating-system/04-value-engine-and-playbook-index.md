# Value Engine and Playbook Index — which document controls

**What this is.** *"Sales Coordinator Operating System • Controlled Index and Consolidation
Guide."* Its stated job:

> *"This index identifies every current Sales Coordinator-related playbook by operating phase,
> defines the owner, trigger, required output, related form or record, and the next process. **It
> also identifies duplicate, overlapping, or potentially conflicting procedures** so the system
> can be governed as one operating model rather than a collection of disconnected documents."*

**Why it matters.** §5 is a **conflict register with a stated controlling rule for each conflict**
— the same job the vault's own conflict stubs do. It is the only TCG document in the drop that
admits its process documentation contradicts itself and says which side wins.

---

## 1. How to use it, in the document's own words

- *"Use the **phase index** to locate the governing playbook for the work being performed."*
- *"Use the **owner and trigger** fields to determine who must act and when the playbook becomes
  active."*
- *"Use the **required output and related form** fields as the completion standard. **A process is
  not complete until its required output exists in the designated system of record.**"*
- *"Use the **next-process** field to prevent work from stopping between departments."*
- *"Use the **overlap register** to determine which document controls when two documents address
  the same activity."*

## 2. Ten operating phases

| # | Phase | Primary control objective |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lead & Opportunity Intake | Identify customer status, gather intake facts, establish records |
| 2 | **Quote Development** | Create, review, approve and issue **commercially accurate** quotes |
| 3 | Technical Planning | Validate crane selection and prepare required lift plans |
| 4 | Aviation & Permit Controls | Complete FAA, NOTAM, permit and waiver requirements |
| 5 | Insurance & Contract Controls | Clear contracts, purchase orders, COIs and endorsements |
| 6 | Labor Compliance | Identify and **release** prevailing-wage, Davis-Bacon and certified-payroll requirements |
| 7 | Scheduling Readiness | Confirm all prerequisites and prepare the complete scheduling package |
| 8 | Dispatch Handoff | Transfer complete, usable information to Dispatch and resolve exceptions |
| 9 | Pre-Job Confirmation | Confirm customer readiness and **control schedule changes before mobilization** |
| 10 | Job Closeout & Finance | Complete supporting records, expense routing and invoice adjustments |

**These ten phases are a third numbering of the same workflow** — 12 backbone steps in the
architecture, 13 stages in the process map, 10 phases here. They cover the same ground with
different boundaries.

## 4. System-of-record standards

| Record type | System of record | Minimum standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer and quote data | **FCC / NextGen** | Customer record, quote number, scope, rates, notes and status **must match the issued quote** |
| Customer communications | Outlook **and** HubSpot | Material instructions, approvals, confirmations and changes retained or logged |
| Scheduling status | Approved Scheduling Sheet / Dispatch system | **"The handoff is not complete until Dispatch accepts the scheduling package."** |
| Technical plans | Controlled project folder / approved planning system | **Current revision, submission date and approval status must be identifiable** |
| Compliance records | Controlled compliance folder and applicable payroll system | Funding classification, wage determination, packet and reporting identifiers retained |
| Contracts, POs and insurance | Controlled customer/job folder | Current approved documents **and exceptions** available before release |
| Commercial approvals | Commercial decision record | Discounts, concessions and invoice adjustments require a **documented approval trail** |

## 5. The duplicate and overlap register

**The most useful table in the governance set.** Ten known conflicts, each with the controlling
rule TCG has chosen.

| Documents | Overlap | **Controlling rule** | Recommended action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creating Quotes in FCC Playbook **+** Creating Quotes SOP | Both govern quote preparation and revision | **FCC Playbook controls system-entry steps. Creating Quotes SOP controls business rules, quality review and customer-facing output.** | Keep both; cross-reference; do not repeat screen-by-screen instructions in the SOP |
| 3D Lift Plan **+** Lift Plan Submission **+** Post-Scheduling Lift Plan Completion | All address lift planning at different maturity points | **3D Lift Plan = preliminary planning. Lift Plan Submission = formal customer/GC submission. Post-Scheduling = the exception where scheduling precedes completion.** | Keep three; define entry and exit criteria on page one of each |
| NOTAMS Playbook **+** NOTAMS(1) | **Likely duplicate versions of the same process** | Newest approved version controls | Archive the older after content comparison and manager approval |
| COI Request Playbook **+** Contracts and COI SOP | Both address insurance requirements | **COI Playbook = request/issuance mechanics. Contracts and COI SOP = contract review, routing and overall clearance.** | Keep both; remove duplicated explanatory sections |
| Ready to Schedule **+** Scheduling Sheet **+** Scheduling Value Engine | All govern movement into Dispatch | **Ready to Schedule = prerequisites. Scheduling Sheet = the record. Value Engine = the full transaction, KPIs and accountability.** | Keep all three; **make the Value Engine the umbrella document** |
| Master Process Map **+** Sales Coordinator Operations Manual | Both cover the end-to-end workflow | **Process Map controls sequence and decision gates. Operations Manual controls detailed execution, daily rhythm and performance standards.** | Keep both; do not duplicate full procedures in the map |
| Day-Prior Automatic Email **+** v2 | **Two versions of the same playbook exist** | v2 controls unless management identifies v1 content to retain | Archive v1 after final comparison |
| Labor Compliance Form SOP **+** Davis-Bacon **+** Certified Payroll | All touch public-work labor obligations | **Form SOP = intake/routing. Davis-Bacon = federal classification. Certified Payroll = reporting setup and execution.** | Keep separate; **add a single shared labor-compliance decision tree** |
| Commercial Decision Playbook **+** Invoice Adjustment Request | Both govern financial concessions | **Commercial Decision Playbook controls approval logic. Invoice Adjustment Request is the transaction form used after invoicing.** | Keep both; **require the form to cite the approving commercial decision** |
| New Customer Playbook **+** New Customer Packet | Playbook and form serve the same phase | **Playbook controls process; packet captures data.** | Keep both; label the packet as the required controlled form |

## 6. Governance actions, with owners and deadlines

| Priority | Action | Owner | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Approve one controlling version where duplicates exist: **NOTAMS and Day-Prior Email** | Sales Manager / Process Owner | 30 days |
| 1 | Add **document-control blocks** to every playbook: owner, version, effective date, approval, superseded document | Operations / HR Document Control | 30 days |
| 1 | Make the **Master Process Map the sequencing authority** and the **Operations Manual the execution authority** | Sales Manager | **Immediate** |
| 2 | Create shared **decision trees** for labor compliance, lift planning and permit/FAA requirements | SC Lead + Functional Owners | 45 days |
| 2 | Standardize exact names for forms, folders and **system statuses** across all documents | Operations | 45 days |
| 2 | Add hyperlinks or document IDs from each playbook to the next governed process | Document Control | 60 days |
| 3 | Quarterly document audit for obsolete, conflicting or unused playbooks | Sales Manager / Quality | Quarterly |

## 7. Naming standard and index control rules

**Recommended file name:** `TCG_[Department]_[Process Name]_[Document Type]_v[Major.Minor]_[Effective Date].docx`
Example: `TCG_SalesOps_Ready-to-Schedule_Playbook_v1.0_2026-08-01.docx`

Document-type vocabulary: **Playbook** = detailed procedure · **Form** = data capture ·
**Process Map** = sequence · **Manual** = role execution · **Value Engine** = an end-to-end
measurable operating system.

**Index control rules**, verbatim:

1. *"A playbook is active only when its **owner, version and approval status** are identified."*
2. *"**A form is not a playbook.** The playbook explains when and how the form is used."*
3. *"A process map does not replace detailed procedures. It establishes sequence, gates and
   handoffs."*
4. *"Where two documents conflict, **the approved controlling rule in this index governs** until
   the documents are corrected."*
5. *"Obsolete versions must be archived and clearly marked **'Superseded — Do Not Use.'**"*
6. *"The index should be reviewed whenever a playbook is added, revised, retired or reassigned."*

**Rule 1 disqualifies most of the set by its own test.** The Creating Quotes and COI playbooks
have blank Playbook Owner fields, and this index's own approval block — Sales Manager, Operations
Leader, HR / Document Control — is **unsigned and undated**.
