**[[quotations/quotations-INDEX|Quotation]] flow :**

Step 1: Lead/quotation request is received (phone, email, website, referral, existing [[customers/customers-INDEX|customer]], sales representative, etc.).

Step 2: Sales team reviews the customer's request.

Step 3: Contacts the customer and gathers project requirements.

Step 4: Decide whether a **Job Walk** is required

* If required, schedule and complete the Job Walk.
* Record site notes, photos, lift requirements, measurements, access restrictions, and safety observations.

Step 5: Create a new quotation.

Step 6: Select or create the Customer and Job Site.

Step 7: Enter general quotation details (description, tentative schedule, customer contact, notes, etc.).

Step 8: Add Resources (cranes, trucks, operators, labour, support equipment).

Step 9: Add [[assets/tool-and-rigging|Tools & Rigging]].

Step 10: Enter Lift Information.

Step 11: Upload Attachments (plans, drawings, photos, documents, etc.).

Step 12: Identify [[projects-jobs/project-module/05-create-and-track-permits|permit]] or traffic control requirements (if applicable).

Step 16: Quotation is automatically saved as a Draft while being prepared.

Step 17: System validates all required information.

Step 18: Check whether internal approval is required (e.g., high-value quotation or special pricing).

Step 19: Manager reviews the quotation.

* Approve
* Reject
* Request changes

Step 20: Once approved internally, the quotation becomes available to send to the customer.

Step 21: Send quotations to the customer via Email or DocuSign.

Step 22: System tracks all communication (sent date, reminders, email replies, signature status, communication history).

Step 23: Customer reviews the quotation.

* Approves
* Rejects
* Requests changes

Step 24: If changes are requested, update the quotation, create a new revision/version, and resend it to the customer.

Step 25: Customer approves and signs the final quotation.

Step 26: If the quotation is rejected, record the Lost Reason and comments.

Step 27: Approved quotation is converted into a Project.
